# SEO Improvements Summary

This document outlines all the SEO enhancements made to your Urgent Plumbing website.

## ✅ Completed SEO Enhancements

### 1. **Enhanced Meta Tags**
- ✅ **Improved Title Tag**: More descriptive with key phrases "24/7 Emergency Plumbing Services | Licensed & Insured"
- ✅ **Extended Meta Description**: 160+ characters with call-to-action and key services
- ✅ **Keywords Meta Tag**: Comprehensive plumbing-related keywords
- ✅ **Author Meta Tag**: Brand identification
- ✅ **Robots Meta Tag**: Explicitly set to "index, follow"
- ✅ **Language Meta Tag**: Set to English
- ✅ **Revisit-After Meta Tag**: Tells search engines to crawl weekly

### 2. **Canonical URL**
- ✅ Added canonical URL to prevent duplicate content issues
- ✅ Points to: `https://urgentplumbing.uk/`

### 3. **Open Graph Tags (Facebook/LinkedIn)**
- ✅ Complete Open Graph implementation for social media sharing
- ✅ Includes: type, url, title, description, image, site_name, locale
- ✅ Optimized for UK market (en_GB locale)

### 4. **Twitter Card Tags**
- ✅ Twitter Card metadata for rich social media previews
- ✅ Large image card format for better visibility
- ✅ All Twitter-specific meta tags included

### 5. **JSON-LD Structured Data (Schema.org)**
Implemented three types of structured data:

#### A. **PlumbingService Schema**
- ✅ Business name, contact information
- ✅ Service types (Emergency Repairs, Installation, etc.)
- ✅ Operating hours (24/7)
- ✅ Service area (United Kingdom)
- ✅ Aggregate ratings (5 stars, 50 reviews)
- ✅ Social media profiles

#### B. **Organization Schema**
- ✅ Company logo
- ✅ Contact points
- ✅ Social media links

#### C. **BreadcrumbList Schema**
- ✅ Navigation structure for search engines
- ✅ Helps with sitelinks in search results

### 6. **Sitemap Improvements**
- ✅ Removed anchor links (not ideal for sitemaps)
- ✅ Focused on main URL and index.html
- ✅ Proper priority and change frequency settings
- ✅ Updated lastmod dates

### 7. **Accessibility Enhancements**
- ✅ Added `aria-hidden="true"` to all decorative SVG icons
- ✅ Improves screen reader experience
- ✅ Better semantic HTML structure

### 8. **Favicon Reference**
- ✅ Added favicon link in head section
- ✅ Placeholder for `/favicon.ico`

### 5. **Monitor Performance**
- Track rankings for target keywords
- Monitor click-through rates in Google Search Console
- Check social media engagement with link previews
- Review structured data coverage report
